I’ve launched Peak on Microsoft Store — a free Windows utility I built to keep AI usage limits and Codex activity in view.

I wanted a quick way to check what’s left in my usage windows without constantly switching away from my work. Peak puts that information in a compact island at the top of the screen.

It shows Codex and Claude subscription limits and reset times, plus local Codex task activity and reported requests for attention. You can adjust its appearance and choose when it stays visible.

Built with C# and .NET WPF. Free for personal and workplace use, with no Peak account or activation. Peak has no telemetry; its observations stay on your computer.

If you use Codex or Claude Code on Windows, I’d love for you to try it. Tell me what works, what feels confusing, and what you’d like to see next. Live readings need compatible provider CLIs and accounts.
